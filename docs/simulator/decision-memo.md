# Regionizer decision memo

> **Scope of this memo: first pass.** This memo was generated on 2026-11-03 from
> `simulator/sweeps/first-pass/` (9,000 ticks = 5 simulated minutes, 5 seeds per cell). The issue's
> plan is 18,000 ticks and 10 seeds, which `simulator/sweeps/` holds and which reruns this memo at
> full scale. Every number below is a measurement on one machine (Apple M1 Max, .NET 9, Release),
> and nothing here settles an open decision.
>
> What to read before using it:
>
> - **Claim radii come out at the top of each one-at-a-time sweep.** On the default map, swept one
>   class at a time, no radius up to 40 m (infantry), 48 m (ground vehicles) or 32 m (aircraft)
>   fuses two fights. With all three at those values together, though, the `baseline-final` reading
>   of the largest region rises from about 482 players (defaults) to about 842. So they interact.
>   The issue's next step, a grid near the chosen values, is needed before these radii are adopted.
> - **Section size is flagged.** No size keeps the regionizer's bookkeeping p99 within the proposed
>   1 ms budget at 5,000 entities in every seed. Baseline measures 1.1–3.2 ms and One base 0.8–1.9 ms,
>   in lockstep, single-threaded. The issue proposed the budget "revised after the first
>   measurement", so this is the measurement for that decision (and for optimising the bookkeeping).
>   The section size stays at its 16 m default meanwhile. Lockstep readings exclude merge-later
>   work (research D7).
> - **Lane bridging limit is flagged.** On the 600 m lane, even 10 clumped travellers fuse the two
>   fights on more than 1% of ticks in the worst seed. The lane is too short for these radii, or the
>   limit is below 10 travellers.
> - **Claim anchoring was compared at the same section size,** not at equal bookkeeping cost
>   (research D15).
